# Governance

This project uses a **BDFL model** (Benevolent Dictator For Life): the
original author has final say over what gets merged, the project's
direction, and releases.

This is separate from the license (GPLv3, see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The
license governs what you're legally allowed to do with the code; this
document governs how decisions about *this specific repository* get made.

## What this means in practice

- Anyone can fork this project and take it in any direction they want —
  that's what the GPL guarantees.
- Contributions (issues, PRs, discussions) are welcome and genuinely
  wanted — this is meant to grow through outside contributors.
- The maintainer decides what gets merged into the canonical repository,
  including saying no to a technically-correct PR that doesn't fit the
  project's direction (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for what
  that direction is).
- There's no committee, no voting. Disagreements get resolved by the
  maintainer's judgment call, explained, not just asserted.

## Why not a committee model

The project has a specific, narrow point of view (Sections 1-2 of the
original design brief: minimum viable architecture, no execution engine,
personal projects over enterprise) that's easy to erode one "reasonable"
feature at a time. A single decision-maker who can say "that doesn't fit"
is what keeps that shape intact while still being open to everyone's
contributions.

This may change as the project grows — if it does, it'll be announced
here, not silently.
